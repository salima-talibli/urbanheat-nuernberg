03 / ABOUT THE DATA

A map built from real observations.

Transparent methods matter as much as compelling cartography.

01

Satellite imagery

USGS Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level-2 surface temperature for 26 August 2025, converted to degrees Celsius with the published scale and offset and screened using QA_PIXEL flags.

02

Spatial analysis

District zonal statistics and means around OpenStreetMap road segments were calculated in QGIS. Road sampling used 15 m buffers; many sampled values rely on only a few pixels.

03

Interpretation

The temperature is land surface temperature, not ambient air or directly measured roadway temperature. The effective thermal resolution is coarser than the road buffers.

Data: USGS / NASA Landsat; OpenStreetMap contributors; Stadt Nürnberg district boundaries. Analysis and web cartography: UrbanHeat Nürnberg.
